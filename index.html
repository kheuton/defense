How to Rank

Optimal Ranking Score

A natural framework: minimize the expected loss

\mathbf{s}^* = \underset{\mathbf{s}}{\operatorname{arg\,min}} \; \mathbb{E}_{\mathbf{y} \sim p_{\theta}} \!\left[ L( \mathbf{s}, \mathbf{y} ) \right]

Why not just the mean?

\bar{\mathbf{s}} = \mathbb{E}_{p_\theta}[\mathbf{y}]

Define the BPR loss (\mathbf{s} = score vector):

L^{\text{BPR}}(\mathbf{s}, \mathbf{y}) = -\frac{\mathbf{y} \cdot \operatorname{TopKMask}(\mathbf{s}, K)}{\mathbf{y} \cdot \operatorname{TopKMask}(\mathbf{y}, K)}

Sub in L^{\text{BPR}}:

\mathbf{s}^* = \underset{\mathbf{s}}{\operatorname{arg\,min}} \; \mathbb{E}_{\mathbf{y} \sim p_{\theta}} \!\left[ -\frac{\mathbf{y} \cdot \operatorname{TopKMask}(\mathbf{s}, K)}{\mathbf{y} \cdot \operatorname{TopKMask}(\mathbf{y}, K)} \right]

Bound denominator:

\mathbf{s}^* = \underset{\mathbf{s}}{\operatorname{arg\,min}} \; \mathbb{E}_{\mathbf{y} \sim p_{\theta}} \!\left[ - \underbrace{\frac{\mathbf{y} \cdot \operatorname{TopKMask}(\mathbf{s}, K)}{K \cdot U}}_{\leq \;\text{BPR}(\mathbf{s}, \mathbf{y})} \right]

Extreme Upper Bound

U \geq y_i for any i

Linearity of expectation:

\mathbf{s}^* = \underset{\mathbf{s}}{\operatorname{arg\,min}} - \underbrace{\frac{\mathbb{E}_{p_{\theta}}[\mathbf{y}] \cdot \operatorname{TopKMask}(\mathbf{s}, K)}{K \cdot U}}_{\leq \;\text{BPR}(\mathbf{s}, \mathbf{y})}

\mathbf{s} = \mathbb{E}_{p_\theta}[\mathbf{y}] is a valid solution.

\mathbf{s} can be any \mathbf{s} such that

\operatorname{TopKMask}(\mathbf{s}, K) = \operatorname{TopKMask}(\mathbb{E}_{p_\theta}[\mathbf{y}], K)

Tighter bound:

\mathbf{s}^* = \underset{\mathbf{s}}{\operatorname{arg\,min}} - \underbrace{\mathbb{E}_{\mathbf{y} \sim p_{\theta}}\!\left[\frac{\mathbf{y}}{\mathbf{1} \cdot \mathbf{y}}\right] \cdot \operatorname{TopKMask}(\mathbf{s}, K)}_{\leq \;\text{BPR}(\mathbf{s}, \mathbf{y})}

Tighter Bound

\mathbf{1} \cdot \mathbf{y} \geq \mathbf{y} \cdot \operatorname{TopKMask}(\mathbf{y}, K)

The ratio estimator:

\mathbf{s}^* = \mathbb{E}_{\mathbf{y} \sim p_{\theta}}\!\left[\frac{\mathbf{y}}{\mathbf{1} \cdot \mathbf{y}}\right] \approx \frac{1}{M}\sum_{m=1}^{M} \frac{\mathbf{y}^{(m)}}{\mathbf{1} \cdot \mathbf{y}^{(m)}}

\mathbf{s} can be any \mathbf{s} such that

\operatorname{TopKMask}(\mathbf{s}, K) = \operatorname{TopKMask}\!\left(\mathbb{E}_{p_\theta}\!\left[\tfrac{\mathbf{y}}{\mathbf{1} \cdot \mathbf{y}}\right], K\right)
